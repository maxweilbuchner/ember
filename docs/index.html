Privacy Policy

Effective July 27, 2026

Ember collects no data. None. There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party code of any kind. Nothing you put into Ember is ever transmitted to us or to anyone else.

Where your data lives

Everything you create in Ember — people, notes, moments, nudge history — is stored only on your device, protected by iOS's built-in encryption. If you enable the app lock, Ember additionally requires Face ID, Touch ID, or your passcode to open.

Contacts access

If you grant Contacts permission, Ember uses it solely to link the people you choose to their contact card, so it can show their name, photo, and birthday. This lookup happens entirely on your device. Ember never reads the Notes field of your contacts, never modifies your contacts, and never transmits contact data anywhere. You can use Ember fully without granting Contacts access.

On-device intelligence

Ember's writing help (spotting people mentioned in a note, suggesting an opening line) runs on Apple's on-device models. Your notes are processed on your device and are not sent to any cloud service.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No push-notification server is involved.

Your data, your control

You can export everything Ember holds as a zip archive to the Files app at any time, and you can permanently erase all Ember data from within the app (Settings → Delete Everything). Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to hand over, sell, or delete on our side — deleting the app or using Delete Everything removes it completely.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted at this address with an updated effective date. Since Ember's design principle is that your data never leaves your device, any future change would only ever describe that more precisely — not weaken it.
